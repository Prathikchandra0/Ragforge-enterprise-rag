NovaTech Solutions — Information Security Policy
Classification: Internal | Version 4.1 | Effective: January 2026

============================================================
SECTION 1: PASSWORD AND AUTHENTICATION
============================================================

Password Requirements:
All passwords must meet these criteria:
- Minimum 12 characters
- At least one uppercase letter
- At least one lowercase letter
- At least one number
- At least one special character (!@#$%^&*)
- Cannot reuse the last 5 passwords
- Must be changed every 90 days

Two-Factor Authentication (2FA):
2FA is mandatory for all employees on:
- Company email
- GitHub
- AWS Console
- VPN
- HR portal (for accessing payslips and personal data)
Use Google Authenticator or Authy as your 2FA app. SMS-based 2FA is not accepted due to SIM-swap vulnerabilities.

Session Management:
- Idle sessions timeout after 30 minutes for sensitive systems (AWS, HR portal)
- General sessions (Slack, Jira) timeout after 8 hours
- Always lock your laptop when leaving your desk (Cmd+L on Mac)
- Never leave your laptop unattended in public spaces

============================================================
SECTION 2: DATA CLASSIFICATION AND HANDLING
============================================================

Data Classification Levels:
- Public: Marketing materials, blog posts, job listings
- Internal: Company policies, org charts, meeting notes
- Confidential: Customer data, financial reports, employee salaries
- Restricted: Encryption keys, database credentials, security audit findings

Handling Rules by Classification:

Public:
- Can be shared externally without approval
- No encryption required

Internal:
- Share within the company freely
- Do not post on social media or external websites
- Okay to discuss in public places (cafes, trains) at a general level

Confidential:
- Share only on a need-to-know basis within the company
- Must be encrypted when shared externally (use company-approved tools)
- Never share via personal email or WhatsApp
- Print copies must be shredded after use

Restricted:
- Access limited to named individuals only
- Must never leave company-managed systems
- Sharing requires written approval from the CISO
- Access is logged and audited quarterly

============================================================
SECTION 3: DEVICE SECURITY
============================================================

Company Laptops:
- Full disk encryption must be enabled (FileVault on Mac, BitLocker on Windows)
- Automatic OS updates must be enabled
- Company antivirus (CrowdStrike) must be installed and running
- Do not install pirated software
- Do not disable the firewall

Personal Devices:
- Personal phones can access company email and Slack ONLY if they have a screen lock (PIN/biometric)
- Do not store company files on personal devices
- If your personal device is lost or stolen, inform IT immediately

USB and External Storage:
- USB storage devices are blocked on company laptops by default
- If you need to use external storage, request a temporary exception from IT
- Never plug in USB devices found in public places

============================================================
SECTION 4: NETWORK AND REMOTE ACCESS
============================================================

Office Network:
- Connect only to the "NovaTech-Secure" WiFi network (WPA3)
- The "NovaTech-Guest" network is for visitors only — employees should not use it
- Do not connect personal IoT devices (smart speakers, etc.) to the office network

VPN:
- VPN is mandatory when working outside the office
- Use the company-provided WireGuard VPN client
- VPN connects you to the internal network — required for accessing internal tools, databases, and staging environments
- Do not use third-party VPN services for work purposes

Public WiFi:
- Avoid using public WiFi for work. Use your phone's hotspot instead
- If you must use public WiFi, ensure VPN is connected BEFORE accessing any company resources
- Never access banking, email, or sensitive systems on public WiFi without VPN

============================================================
SECTION 5: INCIDENT REPORTING
============================================================

What Counts as a Security Incident:
- Laptop lost or stolen
- Suspected unauthorized access to your accounts
- Phishing email received (even if you didn't click)
- Accidentally sharing confidential data with wrong person
- Finding confidential documents left in public areas
- Suspicious USB device found
- Any unusual account activity (login from unknown location)

How to Report:
1. Immediately inform your manager (verbal or Slack DM)
2. Email security@novatech.in with subject: "SECURITY INCIDENT — [brief description]"
3. Do NOT try to investigate on your own — let the security team handle it
4. Preserve evidence — don't delete emails, messages, or files related to the incident

Response Times:
- Critical incidents (data breach, active attack): Response within 30 minutes
- High priority (lost device, compromised account): Response within 2 hours
- Medium (phishing received, suspicious activity): Response within 24 hours
- Low (policy questions, access requests): Response within 48 hours

Phishing:
- If you receive a suspicious email, DO NOT click any links or download attachments
- Forward the email to phishing@novatech.in
- Mark it as spam/junk
- Common signs of phishing: urgency, misspelled sender domain, requests for passwords, unexpected attachments

============================================================
SECTION 6: ACCEPTABLE USE
============================================================

Internet Usage:
- Company internet is primarily for work purposes
- Limited personal use is acceptable during breaks
- The following are strictly prohibited: downloading copyrighted content (movies, software), accessing explicit content, cryptocurrency mining, running personal businesses on company infrastructure

Software Installation:
- Only install software from the company-approved list (available on Confluence)
- If you need software not on the list, submit a request to IT
- Open-source tools may be used for development after checking the license (MIT, Apache OK; GPL requires IT approval)
- Never install cracked or pirated software — this is grounds for termination

AI Tools:
- ChatGPT, Claude, and similar AI tools may be used for work assistance
- NEVER paste customer data, source code, or confidential information into external AI tools
- Use the company-approved internal AI assistant for sensitive queries
- Code generated by AI must be reviewed with the same rigor as human-written code
